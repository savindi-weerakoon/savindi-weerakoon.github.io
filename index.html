About

Language gave me the questions.
Engineering gave me the tools.

My path into UX research is deliberately interdisciplinary. I earned a First Class BA (Hons) in Translation Studies — years spent analysing how meaning survives (or breaks) between humans and systems — then a Distinction-pass MSc in Information Technology, where my thesis built an AI system that adapts user interfaces to an individual's cognitive abilities, validated with eye-tracking.

That combination shapes how I work: I bring a linguist's rigour to qualitative data — annotation schemes, error taxonomies, corpus methods — and an engineer's fluency to quantitative work and prototyping. I can design a study, build the artefact it needs, run it, and analyse the results without hand-offs.

My current research focus is human-centered AI: trust calibration, automation bias, explainability, and how interfaces should adapt as AI systems become collaborators rather than tools. As a visiting lecturer I also teach research methods — which keeps me honest about communicating findings clearly to non-researchers.

AI-Driven Adaptive UI Generation Based on Cognitive Abilities

Can an interface reshape itself around how each user actually thinks?

My MSc thesis and flagship study. I designed and built an AI system that generates user interfaces adapted to an individual's cognitive abilities and preferences, then validated it with undergraduate participants using eye-tracking to measure how attention and comprehension changed across adaptive and static designs. The work spans the full research arc — literature grounding, instrument design, prototype engineering, participant studies, and statistical analysis — and was accepted to a peer-reviewed international conference.

Role: Lead researcher (MSc thesis, SLIIT) · Published: Weerakoon & Wickramarathne, Int. Conf. on AI-Driven Smart Systems and Ubiquitous Computing (ICAUC 2026), Bengaluru, India

Engagement research Gamification ICAC 2024 · IEEE Xplore · Scopus

EduNova: Adaptive Gamification for Learner Engagement

What keeps digital learners engaged — and what makes them stay?

Co-authored an adaptive, gamification-driven framework for improving learner engagement and retention in digital education, contributing to the framework design and evaluation approach. Peer-reviewed, open-access, and presented at an international computing conference.

Role: Co-author · Int. Conf. on Advancements in Computing (ICAC 2024), Colombo

Human evaluation of AI Error analysis CSCCF 2022

How Well Does Google Translate Actually Work?

Where does AI translation genuinely help users — and where does it quietly fail them?

First-author study evaluating Google Translate's effectiveness for informative translation in the English–Sinhala pair — a low-resource language context where marketing claims and reality diverge. Built a systematic human-evaluation and error-analysis protocol; exactly the discipline needed to evaluate today's AI products against real user needs.

Role: First author · Third China–Sri Lanka Communication and Cooperation Forum, 2022

Corpus methods Quantitative analysis AJHSSR · Open access

Effectiveness of English→Sinhala Subtitling: A Corpus-Based Study

Do subtitles carry meaning across — or just words?

First-author, corpus-based study of subtitle quality, measuring how effectively meaning transfers between languages in real media. Demonstrates large-scale systematic analysis of naturalistic user-facing content — the same muscle used in content research and comprehension studies.

Role: First author · American Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences Research, 2022

Comparative study Developer experience Journal · Under review

Manual vs. Automated Testing in Low-Code/No-Code Environments

How should quality practices adapt when the "developer" no longer writes code?

Comparative analysis of manual and automated testing in low-code/no-code platforms, proposing a hybrid framework. A developer-experience (DX) study at its core — examining how tooling reshapes practitioner workflows.

Role: First author (with D. De Silva) · Under review, peer-reviewed journal
